I'm Zerosec, an offensive security researcher and bug bounty hunter. I look for vulnerabilities in web applications and APIs, report them through bug bounty programs on HackerOne and Yandex, and keep my skills sharp on HackTheBox.

Much of my current research is about the security of AI and LLM applications. I publish that work, along with tools and training material, under the HackAI name.

## What you'll find here

- **[Writeups](../writeups/)**: findings from bug bounty programs and labs, written up once they are fixed and cleared for disclosure.
- **[Notes](../notes/)**: working notes on tools, techniques and things worth remembering.
- **[News](../news/)**: short updates on new research, releases and talks.
